## Phase 2 — Generate (full coverage: 4 Generators + 3 Framers)

### G1 · Combination *(Generator)*

- **Generic:** connect the two coordinates the settled ground already names — "must-wait" (firing-time) **∧** "config-independent." The spine = their **intersection**, not either alone.
- **Focused:** connect prior `2026-07-10_23-03`'s single omission-diff (one must-wait check) with harmony_layer.md's **whole-draft structural properties** (escalation-chain, ring-composition, havuz-convergence, cause-effect). Emergent: omission-diff is not a lone check but the **first member of a family** of config-independent, whole-draft-relational checks. The family is the spine; omission-diff was the visible tip.
- **Contrarian:** connect the spine with the **Tier system** (DF4). What if the spine ≈ "Tier-1 meaning-carrying entries **+** the hard-constraints, filtered to *whole-draft-relational*"? If so, the spine is largely *derivable from metadata that already exists* → sharpens the "small prize": firing-time's job is a **filter/relabel over existing Tier + hard-constraint content**, not a new corpus.

### G2 · Absence Recognition *(Generator)* — both levels mandatory

- **Patch-level (gap in current design):** the prior `2026-07-11_00-24` Pass-3 gate carries **only omission-diff**; the **whole-draft structural-fidelity checks** (escalation / ring / havuz / cause-effect) are absent from it. That gap is the spine's *genuinely new content*.
- **Redesign-level (what's missing if built from scratch):** the SKILL nowhere **labels a check as config-independent (always-run) vs config-keyed (A1–A8-derived)**. Designed from scratch, Pass-3 would separate the invariant checks from the parameterized ones. That label is the design's organizing move.
- **Redesign-level, bidirectional (already-present-in-different-form):** the SKILL **already half-has this** — SKILL.md's always-on **"no-smoothing"** policy + harmony_layer.md's **hard constraints** (no split, forbidden merges) ARE config-independent always-run checks, in *narrative* form. → The spine largely **articulates what is already latent**, not invents. (Honest-novelty guard: this is why the prize is small.)

### G3 · Domain Transfer *(Generator)* — native + deliberately-different sources

- **Native (software — source-domain guard):** **invariants vs feature-flag-gated tests.** Config-independent checks = **invariants** (assert on every build regardless of settings, verified against the finished artifact); config-derived checks = **parameterized/flag-gated tests** (run those the active config selects). The spine = the *invariant suite*; the prior's config-derived agenda = the *parameterized suite*. Load-bearing: an invariant is *exactly* "must hold regardless of config, checked against the finished artifact" — the two-part criterion, already named in another field.
- **Deliberately-different (manufacturing QA):** **final-inspection checklist vs in-process station spec.** Integrity/safety checks run on **every unit at final assembly** (must-wait, variant-independent); cosmetic/fit checks are in-process and product-variant-specific. The spine = the every-unit final-inspection set.
- **Contrarian (law — mandatory vs negotiated terms):** mandatory terms bind **regardless of the parties** (config-independent, always in); negotiated terms vary per deal (config-derived). Tests C3 scope: the spine is the set of *mandatory* correspondences between source and draft.

> **Convergence flag:** G1-generic, G3-native (invariants), and F3-inversion (below) independently land on the **intersection-cell / invariant-suite** model. Three mechanisms, one core → high confidence.

### G4 · Extrapolation *(Generator)*

- **Generic:** the corpus grows (new "On X" detection principles accrue over time). An **untagged** Pass-3 agenda would grow unboundedly; the **config-independent spine stays small and stable** (invariants don't proliferate the way detection heuristics do). → the spine's relative value *rises* as the corpus grows; it is the stable core.
- **Focused:** extrapolate the **metadata-theater tax**: tag-all-principles now → every future principle demands a tag → maintenance cost compounds. Only the **derived-set** design (criterion-as-rationale, nothing tagged per-principle) scales. Argues for expression-candidate (c) below.
- **Contrarian:** extrapolate the *other* way — as tooling matures, the near-mechanical spine checks (omission-diff especially) get **scripted out of the prose SKILL into a checker**, leaving Pass-3's prose to the config-derived judgment calls. The spine **migrates to tooling** (connects to prior `2026-07-10_23-03`'s "mechanical omission-diff" + a future validation route). → RESEARCH FRONTIER, not this inquiry.

### F1 · Lens Shifting *(Framer)*

- **Generic:** view the corpus under **"true for ANY reader" vs "true for THIS reader (A1–A8)."** The spine = *reader-invariant fidelity*; the agenda = *reader-relative fluency*. Reframes the whole split as **invariant / relative**, not first-pass / second-pass.
- **Focused:** re-confirm the settled **possibility→necessity** reliability lens at design time — "can-fire-second-pass" (≈everything, useless as a checklist) vs **"must-wait-second-pass"** (few, the checklist). The lens holds: only the reliability reading yields a small enough set to *be* a Pass-3 agenda.
- **Contrarian (the mandated reframe, C6):** shift the lens entirely off *firing-time* onto *config-dependence*. Under this lens, the useful classifier is **config-independent vs config-derived**, and firing-time looks like a *proxy*. (Adjudicated at F3-Inversion + the Inherited Frame Audit.)

### F2 · Constraint Manipulation *(Framer)* — both directions mandatory

- **ADD:** add the constraint **"no per-principle metadata is allowed."** → forces the **derived-set / criterion-as-rationale** design (expression-candidate c): the spine is a *static list* whose membership is *justified by* the criterion, with **nothing tagged on individual principles**. Kills the boolean-attribute design (candidate b) as metadata-theater. The lean design is precisely *what survives this constraint*.
- **REMOVE:** remove the constraint **"the spine must live in SKILL prose."** → the spine could be a **separate machine-checkable artifact** (a checklist file, or a script running the omission-diff). Opens the tooling route (convergent with G4-contrarian). Out of scope here; preserved as frontier.

### F3 · Inversion *(Framer)* — piece-level (property i, ii) + Intervention-Shape-Axis (property v), depth-checked

**(a) Content/framing-axis inversion — the mandated reframe (C6).**
- *Assumption reversed:* "**firing-time** is the classifying axis the surviving contribution is built on."
- *Level-1 (component):* "use **config-dependence** as the classifier instead of firing-time."
- *Level-2 (system):* they are **two orthogonal axes**. The spine is the **intersection cell (must-wait ∧ config-independent)**; neither axis alone defines it. Firing-time supplies **placement** (this is a Pass-3 *check*, not a Pass-1 *hold*); config-independence supplies the **novelty/value** (it *generalizes* the prior omission-diff and *complements* the config-derived agenda — the priors did not have this axis).
- *Multi-axis check — existence-axis:* could the count of *genuinely new* firing-time-derived checks be **≈ZERO**? Nearly: omission-diff already exists (prior); no-add / no-merge-split already exist (harmony_layer hard constraints); only the **whole-draft structural checks** (escalation / ring / havuz / cause-effect) are new content — and those arguably descend from **Tier-1 meaning-carrying harmony**, not from firing-time reasoning. → The honest floor: the spine's novel *content* is near-zero; its yield is the **organizing bipartition** (invariant vs parameterized), not a pile of new checks.
- *Verdict:* firing-time is **not a mere proxy** (it is one of the two real coordinates) but is **not the value-carrying axis** (config-independence is). Both survive; the headline must state the **two-axis** resolution, not "firing-time is the contribution."

**(b) Intervention-Shape-Axis inversion — MANDATORY (property v fires).**
- *Committed shape X = **ADD-CONTENT*** (append the config-independent spine + its criterion-rationale to Pass-3 of harmony_layer.md).
- *Alternative shape Y = **REORGANIZE-WITHOUT-ADDING***: don't add a new block — **re-partition the existing Pass-3 items** into two labeled groups (config-independent / config-derived). *What follows:* since the prior's Pass-3 already lists omission-diff (and harmony_layer already lists the hard constraints), the "spine" is mostly a **relabel + a small addition** (the whole-draft structural checks), NOT a large ADD. Y exposes the true delta as small — consistent with "small prize."
- *Alternative shape Z = **ADD-TEST***: frame the spine as a *must-pass gate* rather than prose content. Near-equivalent here, since Pass-3 already *is* the check gate.
- *Test both/all:* X is right for the **new** whole-draft structural checks + the criterion note; Y is right for the **relabel** of what already exists. → **Committed shape = HYBRID: REORGANIZE the existing Pass-3 into (config-independent spine | config-derived agenda) + ADD-CONTENT only the whole-draft structural checks + the one-line criterion-rationale.** This is more honest than pure ADD-CONTENT and is the refined intervention.
