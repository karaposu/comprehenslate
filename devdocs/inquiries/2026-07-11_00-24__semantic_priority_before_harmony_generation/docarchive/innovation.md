## Phase 2 — Generate (full coverage: 4 Generators + 3 Framers)

The design space has five orthogonal axes (surfaced for the Axis Coverage Check): **(1) wiring locus** — `SKILL.md` Step 5 vs `harmony_layer.md` vs `schemas.py`; **(2) intermediate-artifact reality** — real emitted gloss ↔ simulated in-context sequence; **(3) meaning-lock granularity** — word/clause (transient) vs sentence vs supra-sentential; **(4) method/policy separation** — restructure `harmony_layer.md` or not; **(5) enforcement strength** — advisory ↔ dependency-gated ↔ hard deferred-emit gate.

### G1 · Combination (Generator)

- **Generic:** 3-Pass method × Step 5's flat apply-bag → **replace the bag with the 3-Pass as the spine, and hang each bag-element off its home pass.** `principles` + comprehension govern Pass 1; the config's harmony axes (A1–A8) govern Pass 2 (mapping) and Pass 3 (reconstruction); the Tier 1-4 list governs Pass 3's sacrifice-order. Nothing is lost; everything gets a phase-address instead of being applied "all at once."
- **Focused:** Meaning-lock gloss × the prior inquiry's mechanical source↔draft omission-diff → **one artifact, two gates.** The accurate-but-choppy gloss produced by Pass 1 IS the reference the prior inquiry's post-draft diff runs against at Pass 3. The prior fix always needed a "source of truth" to diff the fluent draft against; the meaning-lock gloss is exactly that object. This is the concrete UNIFICATION the brief asks for: a single 3-Pass wiring produces the meaning-lock-first gate (Pass 1) *and* the post-draft check (Pass 3 diffs against Pass 1).
- **Contrarian:** harmony config (A1–A8) × Meaning Lock → **the disease-reintroducing combination.** If the config is fed INTO Pass 1 (config-aware comprehension), harmony nudges leak back into the semantic layer — exactly the failure. Reversing the combination yields a hard design rule: **Pass 1 must be config-BLIND.** Comprehension locks meaning without seeing the harmony axes; the config enters only at Pass 2/3. (Non-obvious; strongly downstream-load-bearing.)

### G2 · Absence Recognition (Generator)

- **Patch-level absence:** there is no *slot* for a locked-meaning output. Step 5 emits one thing (the translation); `PipelineConfig` in `schemas.py` has chunking/parallel/output knobs but **no meaning-lock stage**. Patch: add the slot (an intermediate the workflow can name and Pass 3 can read).
- **Redesign-level (from scratch):** if comprehenslate were built from scratch around `translation_principals.md` line 16 ("two-step process: comprehend first, then validate"), **the workflow's top-level step structure would BE the two steps** — Step 5 would already read "5a: comprehend & lock meaning / 5b: reconstruct under harmony," not "produce the translation." The structural absence: the executable workflow's shape does not mirror the method its own references describe.
- **Redesign, bidirectional (already-present-in-less-articulated-form):** neither gate is new. The meaning-lock gate is **fully present in narrative** (harmony_layer.md Pass 1); the post-draft check is **half-present** (the prior inquiry already designed it). So the intervention's true shape is **PROMOTION (narrative → invoked procedure), not AUTHORING.** This is the sharpest re-framing of the fix and confirms the "cheaper than new machinery" reading — with one caveat surfaced by the Audit below.

### G3 · Domain Transfer (Generator)

- **Native-domain source (source-domain guard — computing-native):** **the compiler.** A compiler never lowers source → target in one motion; it builds an **Intermediate Representation (IR)** — semantically complete, target-agnostic — in the front-end, then the back-end lowers IR → target code. Transfer: **Meaning Lock = build the IR** (accurate-but-choppy, target-agnostic semantic representation); **Harmony Map + Target Reconstruction = the back-end** that lowers the IR to fluent English under config. The compiler's discipline is precisely the SKILL's missing gate: *you may not skip the IR.* The 7 errors are the signature of source→fluent-target lowering with no IR. And a compiler's IR is **real and emitted**, not "held in mind" — evidence for the real-artifact end of Axis 2.
- **Deliberately-different source:** **contract drafting.** Negotiators freeze the *term sheet* (the deal's meaning) before lawyers draft prose; drafting may polish language but may not silently alter a term. Transfer: freeze the semantic "term sheet" (meaning-lock) first; draft target prose second; prose changes HOW, never WHAT — Pass-3 verbatim.
- **Contrarian/second different source:** **film dubbing / subtitling** — a domain where harmony (lip-sync, timing) *legitimately* overrides meaning; lines are cut and altered to fit. This is the counter-case. Does Nursî-translation resemble dubbing? **No** — the Tier system makes Tier-1 meaning "non-negotiable." So the transfer confirms by contrast: comprehenslate is a **contract** domain (meaning frozen), not a **dubbing** domain (meaning negotiable). Defends semantic priority against the "sometimes harmony should win" objection.

### G4 · Extrapolation (Generator)

- **Trend (do-nothing):** if Step 5 stays a flat apply-bag, then as the harmony layer *improves* (more axes, stronger nudges), fluency-capture gets **worse** — more harmonic pressure on the same un-gated production node. The un-gated design **degrades as harmony strengthens.** So wiring the 3-Pass is not optional polish; it is the precondition for safely extending the harmony layer at all.
- **Trend (fix in place):** with a real meaning-lock IR, downstream features become possible that are impossible today — per-pass QA, translation memory keyed on locked meanings, config A/B-testing at Pass 3 with Pass 1 held fixed. The IR becomes a reusable asset. (Fertility signal.)

### F1 · Lens Shifting (Framer)

- **Generic:** shift from "the SKILL is *missing an instruction*" to "the SKILL has a *dormant method the workflow doesn't call*." Under the dormant-method lens the fix is a wiring change (call it), not authoring — validating the cheap reading.
- **Focused:** shift from "translation is one production act" to "translation is a **pipeline with a meaning-gate**." Under the pipeline lens, Step 5 must name discrete phases with a gate between them; the ordering becomes executable, not descriptive.
- **Contrarian:** shift to "**'passes in one context' is a fiction.**" A model told to "run three passes" in a single generation still generates forward in one motion — the passes collapse exactly as they did (this is *what already happened*: the 3-Pass "ran in spirit as one motion," per the prior finding). Under this lens, sequencing instructions in one prompt is insufficient; only a **real artifact boundary** (emit the meaning-lock, then reconstruct from it) forces the separation. Pushes hard toward the real-intermediate end of Axis 2 and directly challenges a naïve "just invoke it" fix. → escalated to the Inherited Frame Audit.

### F2 · Inversion (Framer)

- **Level 1 (component):** "the workflow should invoke the 3-Pass" → "the workflow should not be *able to avoid* the 3-Pass." A skippable instruction is skipped in one fluent motion; the fix must remove the un-gated path, not add an ignorable step.
- **Level 2 (system):** "Step 5 *produces the translation*" → "**Step 5 does NOT produce the translation** — it produces a meaning-lock; a *later* node produces the translation, and the meaning-lock is its only legal input." System-level architecture: translation is not a step, it is an **edge between two nodes** (lock → reconstruct). The single-production-node design *is* the bug.
- **Multi-axis (existence-axis):** could the count of intermediate artifacts be ZERO rather than N? The "translation is holistic / meaning and harmony are inseparable" claim says zero. But the 7 errors are meaning-losses that harmony did **not** require (dropped clause, wrong word-sense) — meaning and harmony *are* separable here. So N ≥ 1 is warranted; the holistic objection fails. Defends the whole 3-Pass model.

- **Intervention-Shape-Axis Inversion (property (v) — MANDATORY):** Piece C's principal candidate commits shape **REPAIR** (`SKILL.md` Step 5: change its semantics from flat-apply → ordered gated passes) **+ REORGANIZE-WITHOUT-ADDING** (`harmony_layer.md`: separate the 3-Pass method from the Tier policy). Reversed assumption = *the shape is REPAIR/REORGANIZE.* Named alternative shapes from the vocabulary:
  - **ADD-CONTENT** (append "run the 3-Pass" to Step 5 without changing the flat bag). What follows: low risk, but the flat-apply path still exists beside it → skippable in one fluent motion → **the disease recurs.** ADD is *why* the original design fails; adding another applyable item to a bag that is already ignored changes nothing.
  - **ADD-TEST** (append only the post-draft check — the prior inquiry's shape — without the meaning-lock-first gate). What follows: catches some omissions after the fact but never builds the locked foundation; the *first* gate stays missing; fluency still drives generation and the check only mops up.
  - **Verdict:** REPAIR+REORGANIZE dominates precisely because it **removes the un-gated path** (the flat bag stops existing) rather than adding an ignorable sibling to it. Both tested below.

### F3 · Constraint Manipulation (Framer)

- **ADD-direction (1):** add "**Pass 3 may not emit target text for a unit until a Pass-1 meaning-lock for that unit exists.**" The ordering becomes enforced by *dependency*, not by instruction-obedience — the fluent-motion shortcut is structurally blocked.
- **ADD-direction (2):** add "**Pass 1 is config-blind**" (from G1-contrarian) — the meaning-lock is produced without the harmony axes in view, so harmony cannot back-contaminate comprehension.
- **REMOVE-direction:** remove the implicit constraint "**the translation is produced as one output block.**" Removing the single-output assumption is what *permits* a real intermediate artifact (the emitted gloss). Flag on removability: if single-output is non-removable (cost/latency/UX reasons), then the intermediate must be **simulated**, and enforcement must shift from a dependency-gate to a **self-attestation gate** (Pass 3 must state the locked meaning for each unit before reconstructing it). So REMOVE cleanly surfaces the real-vs-simulated tradeoff as a *constraint-removability* decision, not an aesthetic one.

## Inherited Frame Audit (between Phase 2 and Phase 3)

**Step (i) — Seed's central assumption:** *"The fix invokes the existing 3-Pass — cheaply — rather than authoring new machinery; one wiring delivers both gates."* Type: **multi-type** (Design-choice + Belief).

**Step (iii) — Challenge scan:** does any candidate explicitly challenge it? **YES, multiply** — F1-contrarian ("passes in one context is a fiction"), F2-Level-2 ("Step 5 should NOT produce the translation"), F3-REMOVE ("remove the one-output constraint"). The assumption is challenged, so the audit's firing condition (Step iv: *any* assumption un-challenged) is **not** met at the coarse level. **But** the tie-breaker for multi-type assumptions mandates richer treatment, and the challenges converge on a specific, load-bearing refinement worth force-surfacing:

**The refinement the challenges expose.** "Invoke the existing 3-Pass" is under-specified in a way that would let the fix fail. The 3-Pass in `harmony_layer.md` is **itself written descriptively, not imperatively** — surfacing item #3: *"The idea is to create a translation mode that works in three passes"* (framing prose, not a gated instruction). So a fix that merely points Step 5 at "harmony_layer.md's 3-Pass" **imports the same descriptive-not-executable prose** that is already being ignored. Applying the multi-type features:

- **Inversion (Belief aspect, system-level):** "the method exists, just call it" → "**the method does not exist as a procedure — only as a description**; calling a description yields a description." The fix must *convert the 3-Pass from descriptive framing to imperative gated steps*, not merely reference it.
- **Absence Recognition redesign-level (Design-choice aspect):** what is present-but-unarticulated? The method's *steps* are present; their *imperativeness and gating* are absent. Promotion must add the imperative mood + the gate, not just the pointer.

**Audit output (new candidate, integrated into the set):** **the fix touches BOTH files, and the harmony_layer.md edit is REPAIR (semantic), not mere REORGANIZE.** (a) `harmony_layer.md`: split METHOD (the 3-Pass) from POLICY (Tier 1-4) *and* rewrite the 3-Pass from "the idea is to create a mode…" into imperative, gated steps; (b) `SKILL.md` Step 5: invoke the now-imperative method as the ordered spine. This is not "author new machinery" (the steps exist) but it is **more than a one-line pointer** — the "cheapest possible" reading of the settled ground is corrected. `Inherited-Frame-Audit: did not fire at firing-condition level (assumption was challenged); tie-breaker richer-treatment applied and surfaced the descriptive-vs-imperative refinement as an ACTIONABLE correction to the cheap reading.`

## Phase 3 — Test (5-test cycle) + Dispositions

**Assembly first (the architecture the survivors form): the "Meaning-Lock IR spine."**
1. **`harmony_layer.md` restructure** — separate METHOD (3-Pass) from POLICY (Tier 1-4); rewrite the 3-Pass as imperative gated steps. *[shape: REPAIR + REORGANIZE]*
2. **`SKILL.md` Step 5 rewire** — replace "produce the translation. Apply: [bag]" with the 3-Pass spine: **Pass 1 Meaning Lock** (config-BLIND; produces an accurate-but-choppy, word/clause-granular gloss = the IR) → **Pass 2 Harmony Map** (config-aware; identify harmonic structure over the locked meaning; no meaning change) → **Pass 3 Target Reconstruction** (rebuild fluent English under config + Tier; meaning held to the IR; the prior inquiry's mechanical source↔draft omission-diff runs here, against the IR).
3. **Intermediate artifact** — REAL but lightweight + transient: the IR gloss is emitted at word/clause grain (so Pass 3 has a fixed reference and the diff has an operand), then discarded after final output. Simulated-with-attestation is the cheaper fallback if emission is too costly.
4. **Enforcement** — dependency-gate (Pass 3 can't emit a unit without that unit's IR); hard deferred-emit is available if the gate proves weak.

| Test | Result on the IR-spine assembly |
|---|---|
| **Novelty** | New *as wiring* — the components exist as prose; the executable spine + config-blind Pass 1 + IR-as-diff-operand do not exist in the SKILL. Genuine (promotion is novel relative to current executable state). |
| **Scrutiny survival** | Strongest objection: "same fluency-biased model runs all three passes → it rubber-stamps its own lock." Survives *because* Pass 1 is config-blind and emits a real word/clause IR **before** any fluent English exists — the diff at Pass 3 is mechanical (IR units present in draft?), not a matter of the model re-judging its own fluent output. The real-artifact boundary is what defeats the collapse; the simulated variant survives *weaker* here (hence DEFERRED). |
| **Fertility** | Opens per-pass QA, translation memory keyed on IRs, config A/B with meaning fixed (G4). |
| **Actionability** | Directly actionable — two named file edits with defined content. |
| **Mechanism independence** | Converged from Combination, Domain-Transfer (compiler), Inversion (system-level), Absence (redesign) — four independent groundings, different upstream inputs → robust, not spurious. |

**Dispositions:**
- **ACTIONABLE — the Meaning-Lock IR spine** (items 1–4 above). Multi-mechanism convergent; strong evidence.
- **ACTIONABLE — Pass 1 is config-blind.** Convergence of G1-contrarian + F3-ADD(2). The single most important non-obvious constraint; without it the restructure still lets harmony contaminate comprehension.
- **ACTIONABLE — the descriptive→imperative REPAIR of `harmony_layer.md`'s 3-Pass** (Inherited-Frame-Audit output). Load-bearing: without it, the Step 5 invocation imports non-executable prose. Corrects the "cheapest possible" reading of the settled ground (fix is still *promotion, not authoring*, but touches both files and includes a semantic rewrite, not a one-line pointer).
- **DEFERRED — simulated intermediate (in-context sequence + self-attestation gate).** Single-mechanism (F3-REMOVE flag), cheaper, but re-opens the collapse risk the real artifact closes. *Revival trigger:* if emitting a real IR proves too costly/latency-heavy across the next ~3 translation runs, promote the simulated+attestation variant as the pragmatic default.
- **DEFERRED — `schemas.py` PipelineConfig gains a meaning-lock stage field.** Single-source (Absence patch-level); the ordering can live in `SKILL.md` prose without a schema change. *Revival trigger:* if prose-only ordering fails to fire (a run again collapses the passes), encode the stage in the schema.
- **RESEARCH FRONTIER — real-vs-simulated is ultimately empirical.** Deciding it for real needs matched test translations (one chunk each way, compared for the 7-error signature); exceeds this inquiry's scope. Recorded for the finding's Open Questions.

**Axis Coverage Check:** (1) wiring locus — covered (SKILL.md + harmony_layer.md; schema DEFERRED); (2) intermediate-artifact reality — covered (real ACTIONABLE / simulated DEFERRED); (3) granularity — covered (config-blind word/clause IR, transient; Pass-3 supra-sentential); (4) method/policy separation — covered (REORGANIZE+REPAIR); (5) enforcement strength — covered (dependency-gate ACTIONABLE; hard deferred-emit available). **All five axes have ≥1 variant.** No single-axis collapse.
