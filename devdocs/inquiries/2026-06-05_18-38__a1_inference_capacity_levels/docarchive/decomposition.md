#### **P3 — The 5 individual level specs (P3.1–P3.5)**

Each verifies template instantiation + concrete examples.

##### **P3.1 — `very_basic`**
- Reader: young child / brand-new L2
- Inference-load tier: compression-depth 0; ellipsis 0; anaphora ≤1 sentence; pragmatic literal-only; presupposition minimal; bridging 0
- Register/genre-tier: children's signs / early-reader instructional
- Gap-filling: AGGRESSIVE all 6 primary actions
- Positive examples: explicit-step prose with all connectives ("John went to the restaurant. John saw the waiter. The waiter was rude. Because the waiter was rude, John was unhappy.")
- Negative examples: any implicit-step text (daily Clark-Haviland "John went to the restaurant. The waiter was rude." with implicit "John saw the waiter")

##### **P3.2 — `daily`**
- Reader: functional adult (backpacker / new immigrant)
- Inference-load tier: compression-depth ≤1; ellipsis minimal; anaphora ≤2 sentences; pragmatic obvious Gricean; presupposition common; bridging 1-step (Clark-Haviland)
- Register/genre-tier: practical guides + simple news with explicit connectives
- Gap-filling: MODERATE — BRIDGE-CONNECTIVES + ADD-BRIDGING-INFERENCES + RESOLVE-ANAPHORA when antecedent >2 sentences
- Positive examples: Clark-Haviland-level bridging ("John went to the restaurant. The waiter was rude.")
- Negative examples: 2-step bridging (conversational news-style); academic compression (advanced); Said Nursi-style telescoping (advanced/native)

##### **P3.3 — `conversational`**
- Reader: average newspaper-reading educated adult
- Inference-load tier: compression-depth ≤2; ellipsis VP-ellipsis/gapping; anaphora cross-paragraph; pragmatic standard Gricean; presupposition educated-general; bridging 2-step news-style
- Register/genre-tier: mainstream journalism + popular non-fiction
- Gap-filling: LIGHT — UNPACK-COMPRESSION for academic-rare 3+ step chains; news-style implicit connectives acceptable
- Positive examples: news-style implicit-causal ("The bill passed the Senate yesterday. Critics decried it as rushed." — implicit political-process bridging)
- Negative examples: Said Nursi 5-step (advanced); Henry James suspended inference (native)

##### **P3.4 — `advanced`**
- Reader: university-educated / skilled non-native reading widely
- Inference-load tier: compression-depth ≤3 (Said Nursi 5-step approaches upper bound); ellipsis academic+literary; anaphora long; pragmatic literary-academic; presupposition academic-general incl. literary-canonical; bridging 3-step + suspended-thread
- Register/genre-tier: academic articles + contemporary literary fiction + dense argumentative
- Gap-filling: MINIMAL — UNPACK-COMPRESSION for Said Nursi-style; KEEP-AS-IS most; ADD-PRESUPPOSITION-CONTEXT for A2 specialist
- Positive examples: Said Nursi 5-step (user's seed: "Rahman → Rezzak → Rızk → Beka → Vücud → İlim/İrade/Kudret → Hayat"); academic compressed argument; "He met his Waterloo" (dual-membership cross-ref P6)
- Negative examples: A1.native (5+ step istilzam routine; Henry James suspended inference); A2 specialist (legal precedent; mathematical WLOG)

##### **P3.5 — `native`**
- Reader: educated native reading literary-extreme incl. classical-rare
- Inference-load tier: compression-depth 5+ routine; ellipsis poetic + literary-extreme; anaphora unlimited general; pragmatic full literary-rhetorical; presupposition literary-canonical + archaic + Biblical + classical; bridging 5+ step + non-linear
- Register/genre-tier: ALL general literary incl. Quranic + classical-Arabic + KJV-Pauline + Henry James + Faulkner + Said Nursi
- Gap-filling: NONE for general; KEEP-AS-IS; only A2 specialist gets ADD-PRESUPPOSITION-CONTEXT
- Positive examples: full Said Nursi istilzam chains routine; Henry James suspended inference; Faulkner stream-of-consciousness; classical Arabic compressed argument; "Crossing the Rubicon" (dual-membership cross-ref P6)
- Negative examples (A2 specialist): legal "absent any showing that, by operation of law, the doctrine of precedent compels"; mathematical "WLOG let x ∈ ℝ; by induction"; medical differential-diagnosis chains

- "WLOG (without loss of generality), let x ∈ ℝ..." — universal-generalization presupposition

- "Results suggest..." — data-to-claim conventional inference

**Borderline (humanities-general → A1.native, NOT A2):** academic-humanities inference chains; literary-criticism inference. These are general educated reading, not domain-specialist training.

#### **P7 — Orthogonality with syntactic-processing-capacity (NEW)**

**Question:** How is inference-capacity ORTHOGONAL to syntactic-processing-capacity, and what concrete examples illustrate the independence?

**Verification:**
- [ ] **Orthogonality statement:** parsing dense nested syntax (working-memory load on STRUCTURE — does the reader hold 3 clauses in suspension?) is operationally distinct from filling implicit logical steps (working-memory load on INFERENCE — does the reader fill 3 missing causal-chain steps?). Independent cognitive dimensions.
- [ ] **Concrete divergence examples:**

  **Example 1 — Advanced syntax + daily inference (rare but real):**
  - Reader profile: careful word-by-word parser of Henry James who doesn't follow the argument
  - Text: long Henry-James-style sentence with 3-clause suspension AND simple-explicit argument → reader handles syntax (advanced) but if argument were compressed, reader fails (daily inference)

  **Example 2 — Daily syntax + advanced inference (more common):**
  - Reader profile: proverb-lover; aphorism-reader
  - Text: "Haste makes waste." (simple SVO syntax — DAILY) → but requires inferring causal chain "Acting hastily → reduced attention → errors → wasted effort" (advanced inference)
  - Or: "All that glitters is not gold." (simple syntax) → requires inferring meta-rhetorical "appearances can deceive" (advanced inference)

  **Example 3 — Both at advanced (Henry James / Said Nursi):**
  - Reader profile: educated literary reader
  - Text: literary-extreme prose requiring BOTH suspended parsing AND compressed inference

  **Example 4 — Both at very_basic (children's prose):**
  - Reader profile: early-reader
  - Text: simple SVO + explicit causal links

- [ ] **Implication for composite-axis configuration:** the user can set A1 = `conversational` as headline + syntactic_processing_capacity = `conversational` (default) + inference_capacity = `advanced` (override) for a reader who handles aphoristic compressed argument despite handling only conversational syntax.

- [ ] **Why this is in scope for this inquiry:** the boundary with syntactic-processing-capacity is the most important cross-sub-field clarification because of the apparent similarity (both are about cognitive load); making the orthogonality explicit prevents collapse.
