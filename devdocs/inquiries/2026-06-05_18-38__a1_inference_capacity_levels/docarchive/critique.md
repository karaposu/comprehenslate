- Strongest objection: "Can someone with daily syntax follow advanced inference?" Reply: yes — proverb / aphorism readers. Real.

**Example 3 — Both at advanced:** typical educated literary reader. Real.

Reply: the composite-axis pattern's strength is independent measurement; failing to document orthogonality risks users assuming inference = syntax-capacity by default. The note prevents misconfiguration in edge cases.
