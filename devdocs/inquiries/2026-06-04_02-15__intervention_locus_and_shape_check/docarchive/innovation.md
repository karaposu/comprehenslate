## Phase 2 — Generate

### Mechanism 1 — Lens Shifting (Framer)

**Generic.** Shift "the answer is YES or NO" → "the answer is YES with multi-axis scope choice." The user's binary question shape gets reframed as a multi-dimensional answer because the structural reality is multi-dimensional (file-level + decision-type-level + scope-level loci).

**Focused.** Shift "the user asked about `harmony_layer.md`" → "the user asked about the right INTERVENTION SHAPE; the file is one dimension among several." Reframes the question as locus-shape, not locus-file.

**Contrarian.** Shift "the prior inquiries' recommendation needs validating" → "the prior inquiries' recommendation should be QUESTIONED on whether the 3-doc bounding was a critical error." Out of scope: sensemaking adjudicated at HIGH confidence that the recommendation is additive-not-invalidating; re-litigating wastes effort.

### Mechanism 2 — Combination (Generator)

**Generic.** Combine YES + supplementary-loci → **YES-WITH-CAVEATS** deliverable shape: the answer's STRUCTURE is yes + transparent enumeration of caveats. Maps directly to sensemaking's stabilized model.

**Focused.** Combine each supplementary locus with its decision type → **decision-type-locus mapping table**: term-definition → terminology.md; config → how_config_should_be.md; principle-authority → translation_principals.md (pending). Makes the reasoning per locus visible at a glance.

**Contrarian.** Combine framework-knowledge-gap + user-agency → **transparent surfacing without imposition**: the gap is communicated, the supplementary loci are surfaced, but the user picks scope. No prescriptive expansion.

### Mechanism 3 — Inversion (Framer)

**Level 1.** "The YES needs elaborate justification" → "the YES is structurally obvious." Component-level.

**Level 2 (depth-check).** "Lengthy explanations are needed for confidence" → "minimum justification IS what produces confidence — the answer's structural obviousness is the justification." System-level: the recommendation's correctness is self-evidencing; over-elaboration is performative.

**Level 3.** "The user needs the AI to argue for the YES" → "the user needs the AI to NAME what's obvious + flag what's non-obvious; the non-obvious is the supplementary loci." Root-level shift.

**Multi-axis check.**
- *Existence-axis:* what if the user wants NO supplementary loci? Then Path A (primary-only) is the deliverable; the framework-knowledge-gap is informational only. Already in the scope structure.
- *Identity-axis:* what is the deliverable FUNDAMENTALLY? It's a scope-choice presentation + recommendation, not just a YES/NO. Aligned with sensemaking.

**Three-variation grouping:**
- Generic: add justification (Level 1)
- Focused: TERSE confirmation + structural-obviousness framing (Level 2)
- Contrarian: name-what's-obvious + flag-what's-non-obvious (Level 3)

### Mechanism 4 — Constraint Manipulation (Framer)

**ADD-direction.**
- *Generic ADD:* "every supplementary locus must state its decision-type." Forces decision-type-locus mapping (per Combination focused).
- *Focused ADD:* "the default recommendation must be DEFENSIBLE not arbitrary." Forces explicit reasoning for the default.
- *Contrarian ADD:* "the deliverable must fit on one screen for quick scan." Forces parsimony at deliverable level.

**REMOVE-direction.**
- *Generic REMOVE:* "remove the 5-path structure; just give the default." Loses user agency; rejected.
- *Focused REMOVE:* "remove the framework-knowledge-gap explanation; just present supplementary loci." Loses context for why supplementary loci exist now; rejected.
- *Contrarian REMOVE:* "remove all supplementary loci; just confirm YES." Defeats the inquiry's discovery; rejected.

**Bidirectional compliance:** both directions covered; REMOVE side mostly produces rejections, which itself is signal — the structure has no easily-removable parts.
