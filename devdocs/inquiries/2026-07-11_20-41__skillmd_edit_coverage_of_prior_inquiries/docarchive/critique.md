## Phase 2 — Adversarial Evaluation

### The 04-12 cell — "COVERED-at-the-honest-ceiling" — **[sharpest generosity angle]**
**Prosecution.** This is a EUPHEMISM. 04-12's load-bearing recommendation is "real enforcement needs the ENGINE (separate calls)." SKILL.md is prose. So the SKILL did NOT satisfy 04-12's primary recommendation — it did what a `.md` can. Labeling that "COVERED" (even "at-ceiling") inflates the scorecard: a reader scanning the column sees "COVERED" and infers 04-12 is satisfied, when its load-bearing half (engine-enforcement) is entirely unaddressed. This is the same over-claim the whole inquiry was built to avoid, hiding in the most generous cell.
**Defense.** 04-12's finding *explicitly* framed the SKILL instruction as a legitimate nudge and the engine as a *separate layer* — so doing the nudge IS the SKILL-layer's whole share of 04-12; the engine share was never this artifact's job. "At-ceiling" was meant to carry exactly that.
**Collision.** The prosecution wins on the LABEL. "COVERED" over-credits because 04-12's primary recommendation is not met — it's *out of this artifact's scope*, which is different from *covered*. → **REFINE:** relabel the 04-12 cell **"SKILL-layer share done (prose nudge) / engine share OPEN — out of a `.md`'s scope"** — NOT "COVERED." The scorecard must not read as "04-12 satisfied." (Same relabel tightens S5's honesty: the engine is where 04-12 actually lands.)
**Position:** boundary — right content, over-crediting label.

### B — the DF4 crux verdict "the failure class is NOT yet closed"

**Collision.** Both right on different aspects. → **REFINE B** to the precise split: **"the config-contamination mechanism is NOT closed (5.1 still comprehends with config in view); the one-motion-finalization aspect is PARTIALLY softened (the deferral)."** More accurate than a flat "not closed" — and it *strengthens* the finding by naming exactly what's open vs softened.
**Position:** boundary — right verdict, needs aspect-precision.
