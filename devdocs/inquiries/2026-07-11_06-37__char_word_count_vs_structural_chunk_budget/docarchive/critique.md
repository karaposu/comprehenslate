# Structural Critique — char/word-count vs structural chunk-budget (Inquiry 06-37)

**Burden of proof.** The candidate-space is an *evaluate-and-deepen* inquiry whose reach is gated (deepen-only until authorized), and whose survivors mostly recommend *how to state a claim* rather than *irreversible edits*. Stakes are therefore MIXED: the design pieces (S1–S3) are low-stakes-reversible → innocent-until-proven-guilty; but the **evaluation claims** (B's quarantine-lift, the confound-robustness, the REFINES-04-48 delta) are HIGH-stakes-for-honesty — they are what a future reader will act on, and an overclaim here silently corrupts the whole chain's provenance. For the evaluation claims the burden shifts to **guilty-until-proven-innocent**: the finding must demonstrate the claim is *exactly* as strong as stated, not merely plausible. This split governs the whole critique — it is why the two sharpest prosecutions land on B and on the confound-robustness (evaluation claims), not on S1–S3 (cheap designs).

## Phase 2 — Adversarial Evaluation

### Candidate B (evaluation claim) — "the evidence PARTIALLY LIFTS 04-48's quarantine" — **[angle 1c/2c, SHARPEST]**

**Prosecution.** 04-48 quarantined "~3500 as reasoned-not-measured, zero empirical evidence." B says the measured points *partially lift* that quarantine. But *lift it on what proposition?* Two distinct propositions were quarantined together:
- (i) the **intervention-warrant**: "adopting a size budget ~3500 helps comprehenslation integrity";
- (ii) the **length-mechanism**: "size/length is causally what degrades adherence."

Now run the crutch-hypothesis as a live rival: *if the failure is purely un-wired-method* (the I7 diagnosis), then the two data points are evidence that **the method collapsed on the big text** — i.e., they are METHOD-evidence. Using them to lift the quarantine on a **size**-claim is **borrowing method-evidence to underwrite a length-claim.** The "provenance" (that 3500 = a real good example's source size) establishes only that *3500 is the size of one success* — it does NOT establish that *3500 is a threshold* or that *size is why*. So B, as stated, conflates "this number came from somewhere real" (true) with "this number is empirically warranted as a size-threshold" (false). The killer form: **the differential (small succeeded, big failed) is exactly what a pure-method failure ALSO predicts, if the big text merely happened to contain more method-traps — so the evidence cannot discriminate size-cause from method-cause, and any "lift" that attaches to the size-mechanism is unearned.**

**Defense.** There is a genuine asymmetry the prosecution understates: the SAME un-wired method was in force in BOTH translations. If the method were the *whole* story and size irrelevant, the small text should have failed too. It didn't. That differential is *weak* evidence that size (or something co-varying with size) matters — the un-wired method *survived at small size*. And the intervention-warrant genuinely IS confound-robust (see D3 candidate): a size budget is a cheap bet that pays off whether the cause is load or method-needing-room. So *something* lifts.

**Collision.** The defense rescues the **intervention-warrant** but not the **length-mechanism**. "The un-wired method survived at small size" supports "keep chunks small" (intervention) — it does NOT isolate size as *the cause*, because the small text also differs from the big text in content (it may simply have lacked lisan-ı hal-class traps — the content-confound the defense cannot close at n=2). So the lift is **real on (i), null on (ii).** B's phrasing "partially lifts the quarantine" is true but dangerously unscoped — it reads as lifting the whole quarantine a little, when in fact it lifts one half (the intervention-warrant, weakly) and lifts the other half (the length-mechanism) **not at all.** → **REFINE.**

**Position:** boundary — strong core (the intervention-warrant lift is real), specific weakness on D1/D2 (the claim is unscoped and lets the mechanism-lift ride on the intervention-lift's coattails).

### Candidate D3 (evaluation claim) — the confound-robustness: "a smaller chunk lets ANY method survive" — **[angle 2b, SHARPEST]**

**Prosecution.** The claim is the inquiry's *key survival result*, so it must be tested at full strength. Take the mistakes.md errors literally and classify them by whether chunk-size plausibly helps:
- **lisan-ı hal / lisan-ı kal → gibberish**: a specific idiom mistranslated. In a 500-char chunk the sentence containing it is *still there* and the model *still* has to render it. If the model doesn't know the idiom, a smaller surrounding context does **not** teach it. → **LOCAL / competence-limited — chunking does not help.**
- **iş → "work" (not attention), esbab → "secondary causes", şahıs → "figure"**: word-sense errors. Per the project's own established principle ([[feedback_translation_polysemy]]), word-sense is fixed by *local construction*, not by surrounding-context volume. A smaller chunk contains the same local construction. → **LOCAL — chunking does not obviously help.**
- **register ("allegorical" where the config forbids hard words)**: a per-word discipline drop.
So the "ANY method survive" claim over-reaches: for **competence-limited local errors, a 500-char chunk fails exactly as a 5000-char chunk does.** The confound-robustness was sold as "helps regardless of mechanism" — but it is only robust across the *length-vs-method* pair, and **blind to the third cause (local capability)** the frame-premise test surfaced. For THIS specific failure, an unknown fraction of the errors are plausibly chunk-invariant.

**Defense.** Two real rescues: (a) the confound-robustness does not need "helps ALL errors" — it needs "helps a real subset cheaply." *Register discipline* and other *load-shed* drops (the model *can* comply but stops bothering under juggling) ARE plausibly load-sensitive → a smaller chunk frees capacity to keep the discipline lit. (b) The mechanism for even the "local" errors is itself confounded: maybe the model *does* know lisan-ı hal but drops care under load — in which case chunking DOES help. We cannot prove the local errors are competence-limited any more than the analysis could prove they are load-driven.

**Collision.** The defense converts the claim from *false* to *over-scoped-but-partly-true*. The honest form is not "helps ANY method survive" but **"gives any *load-sensitive* failure more room to survive; for genuinely competence-limited local errors it may do nothing — and which errors are which is itself confounded."** The intervention still survives as a cheap net-positive bet (it helps the load-sensitive subset, and MIGHT help the rest), but the *argument* is weaker and narrower than "confound-robust, helps regardless." → **REFINE.** Critically, this compounds with B: if part of the failure is chunk-invariant local-capability, then even the *intervention-warrant* lift (B-(i)) is thinner than B claims — the intervention is warranted for the load-sensitive share only.

**Position:** boundary — the survival result holds in scoped form; the unscoped form fails D3.

### Candidate S1 — the number = demonstrated-safe 4,345 × ~0.8 "safety factor" ≈ 3,500–4,000 — **[angle 1a]**

**Prosecution.** A safety factor is derived from a *known failure distribution* with a *located boundary*. Here: **n=1 judged-good source (4,345)** and **n=1 judged-failed at 28,330 (6.5× larger)**, with the entire 4,345→28,330 span **unobserved** and the failed text's *source size unknown*. The "0.8" is not derived — it is `3500 / 4345` computed backward from the user's pre-existing number and relabeled. Calling it a "safety factor" imports engineering precision the data has not earned: it implies the failure boundary sits just above 4,345, which is unknown (it could be 6,000 or 20,000). And note 5th_word (4,148) is **unjudged**, so there is really **one** judged-good size, not two.

**Defense.** The *substance* is sound: choosing a working number *below your single demonstrated-safe point* is correct conservatism, and "lower for smaller models" is right. Going *below* 4,345 (rather than at it) is defensible because 4,345 is n=1 and might itself be near a cliff.

**Collision.** Substance survives; the "safety factor" *form* is false precision. → **REFINE:** strip "× 0.8 safety factor"; state as **"3,500 is a conservative pick below the single demonstrated-safe source size (4,345); no failure boundary is located, so no true safety factor is computable — this is 'below-the-one-safe-point,' not 'safe-point × margin.'"** Express per-model as "a smaller fraction of that model's demonstrated-safe point, calibrated as data arrives," and keep the number a **band** (floor ~3,500 / demonstrated-safe ~4,345 / unknown above), not a cliff.

**Position:** boundary — right instinct, over-precise label.

### Candidate S2 — output = symptom-to-MONITOR; expansion-ratio watch, flag >~2× — **[angle 1b]**

**Prosecution.** Two hits. (1) **Redundancy with 01-09:** the whole-draft config-independent mechanical check (01-09) already inspects the produced translation's content; a 2.57×-over-expanded, register-broken draft would be caught *there*. The expansion-ratio is a cruder proxy for what 01-09 does properly — as a standalone quality gate it adds nothing. (2) **Same n=1 false-precision as S1:** the ">2×" threshold is set from ONE failure (2.57×) against a healthy band (1.49–1.63×) that is itself n=2. Three points cannot fix a threshold.

**Defense.** The category is right and the innovation was careful to call output a *symptom to monitor, not a cause to bound* (bounding a generated quantity is a category error — correctly avoided). And the ratio is *cheaper* than 01-09: pure arithmetic on two char-counts, runnable as an automatic tripwire that *escalates* to the fuller 01-09 check rather than replacing it.

**Collision.** Survives only in the demoted form. → **REFINE:** keep the expansion-ratio as a **cheap tripwire that feeds the 01-09 whole-draft check, not a standalone gate**; soften ">2×" to **"materially above the healthy ~1.5–1.6× band, calibrate as data arrives"**; explicitly acknowledge the overlap with 01-09 so it is not double-counted as new QA.

**Position:** boundary — cheap-signal value only, redundant-as-a-gate.

### Candidate S3 — define the untyped `chunking_budget` as source-chars (REPAIR) + instruction-now (ADD-CONTENT), phase-split, gated — **[angle 1d]**

**Prosecution.** (1) The instruction-now — SKILL.md prose "translate in ~3,500-char source-chunks snapping to boundary" — is a **one-run prose instruction**, which the chain's OWN established enforcement gradient ([[project_skill_design_discipline]] Rule 3; 04-12's middleware-vs-instruction; 04-48's staged borders) rates a **weak probability-raiser the model can ignore.** Selling it as the fix would repeat exactly the softness 04-48 already flagged. (2) Typing the field risks being **cosmetic**: a typed `chunking_budget_source_chars: int` that is still un-wired into the pipeline does nothing at runtime.

**Defense.** (1) The innovation did NOT sell instruction-now as the fix — it phase-split: instruction-now as the *cheap immediate* raiser, engine-later (the typed field + `fixed-budget-with-snap` actually chunking) as the *real enforcer*. That is the gradient applied correctly, not violated. (2) Typing is not cosmetic in *documentation* terms: the untyped `int` is precisely what invited the char-vs-token-vs-structure confusion this whole traverse spent itself resolving — typing it *prevents recurrence*. It is **necessary-not-sufficient**: it fixes the spec's honesty without, alone, making chunking happen.

**Collision.** Survives *because* it already carries the honest labels the prosecution demands. → **SURVIVE with caveats:** (a) instruction-now must be stated as a **weak one-run raiser (real enforcement = the engine)**, never as the fix; (b) typing the field is a **necessary-not-sufficient REPAIR** (documentation/anti-confusion value; zero runtime effect until wired); (c) the whole piece stays **gated behind reach + explicit authorization + the engine**, exactly like 04-48's chunking. All three caveats are consistent with what innovation wrote → no refinement needed beyond making the labels explicit in the finding.

**Position:** viable — the phase-split is the correct enforcement-honest shape; caveats are labeling, not structural.

### Candidate S4 — the crutch-vs-permanent RE-TEST TRIGGER — **[angle 1c's positive twin]**

**Prosecution.** Is this just a hedge dressed as an insight — "we don't know, so let's call it undecided"? And does raising it *undercut the whole inquiry* (if the char-budget is *merely* a crutch for the un-wired method, why invest in it)?

Crucially it does NOT undercut adopting the budget *now* — the confound-robustness (even in its scoped form) says the budget helps in the *current* un-wired state regardless.

**Collision.** Defense dominates. This is the candidate that makes the whole inquiry honest rather than tidy. → **SURVIVE (clean, no critical caveat).** It is the strongest candidate on the landscape and should be the finding's intellectual centerpiece — the char-budget is held as **"adopt now (helps regardless); revisit long-run necessity after the 3-Pass is wired."**

**Position:** viable-core.

Stated that way, "REFINES" is honest; stated loosely, it inflates.
